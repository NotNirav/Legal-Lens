# SECTION 3: STATUTORY CONCORDANCE, AMENDMENTS & REPEALS REGISTRY

### 1. The Four New Labour Codes Concordance & Repeals
The Parliament of India consolidated 29 pre-existing central labour laws into four comprehensive Labour Codes:
1. **Code on Wages, 2019 (Act 29 of 2019):**
   - Repeals:
     * Payment of Wages Act, 1936 (4 of 1936)
     * Minimum Wages Act, 1948 (11 of 1948)
     * Payment of Bonus Act, 1965 (21 of 1965)
     * Equal Remuneration Act, 1976 (25 of 1976)
   - Implementing Rules in Corpus: `The Code on Wages (Central) Rules, 2026 (G.S.R. 343(E))`
2. **Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (Act 35 of 2020):**
   - Repeals:
     * Trade Unions Act, 1926 (16 of 1926)
     * Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946 (20 of 1946)
     * Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 (14 of 1947)
3. **Code on Social Security, 2020 (Act 36 of 2020):**
   - Repeals: Employees' Compensation Act 1923, Employees' State Insurance Act 1948, Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952, Maternity Benefit Act 1961, Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, Cine-Workers Welfare Fund Act 1981, Building and Other Construction Workers' Welfare Cess Act 1996, Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act 2008.
4. **Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (Act 37 of 2020):**
   - Repeals: Factories Act 1948, Mines Act 1952, Dock Workers Act 1986, Building and Other Construction Workers Act 1996, Plantations Labour Act 1951, Contract Labour Act 1970, Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act 1979, Working Journalists Acts 1955/1958, Motor Transport Workers Act 1961, Sales Promotion Employees Act 1976, Beedi and Cigar Workers Act 1966.

### 2. Real Estate Legislation Hierarchy & Precedence
- **RERA 2016 vs MOFA 1963:** Section 89 of RERA provides that its provisions shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent contained in any other law for the time being in force. However, provisions of MOFA that are not inconsistent (such as Section 11 Deemed Conveyance of land title to cooperative housing societies) continue to be invoked in Maharashtra.
- **RERA 2016 vs Maharashtra Housing Act 2012:** Maharashtra Act No. II of 2014 was enacted prior to RERA but was expressly repealed pursuant to Section 92 of RERA, establishing RERA as the supreme regulatory framework.
- **Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999 (Act XVIII of 2000):** Expressly repealed the Bombay Rents, Hotel and Lodging House Rates Control Act 1947, the Central Provinces and Berar Regulation of Letting of Accommodation Act 1946, and the Hyderabad Houses (Rent, Eviction and Lease) Control Act 1954 across Maharashtra.

### 3. Criminal Law Modernization (Effective 1 July 2024)
- **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023:** Replaced the Indian Penal Code (IPC), 1860.
- **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023:** Replaced the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), 1973.
- **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023:** Replaced the Indian Evidence Act (IEA), 1872.

### 4. Decriminalization under Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023
- Decriminalized minor procedural lapses and replaced imprisonment with administrative monetary penalties across 42 Central Acts (e.g. IT Act 2000, Environment Protection Act 1986, Air Act 1981, Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940, Copyright Act 1957, Patents Act 1970).
